## The theory in brief

1. **Jhana is an ignition, not an amount.** First jhana starts when a feedback loop between
   attention and a pleasant feeling becomes self-sustaining. Attention amplifies the feeling;
   the feeling captures attention, relaxes the body and quiets the mind.
2. **The loop has a gain.** It rises with continuous attention on the feeling, bodily ease and
   sensitivity. It falls with natural fading and with grasping. Above 1 the feeling grows by
   itself; the take-off is sudden because the gain rises as the feeling does.
3. **Interference is the usual binding constraint.** Straining, grabbing at the feeling when it
   starts, checking on it, bracing against it. This is why the instruction at the critical
   moment is "do nothing" in every fast-working lineage, and why years of effortful practice do
   not predict success.
4. **Six conditions**: a seed, continuity, ease, non-interference at the onset, brightness, and
   enough time. Possibly a seventh: permission to feel good.
5. **People differ in which condition binds**, and the fixes are opposites. A coach's main job
   is to find it. Retreats work through dose plus diagnosis.
6. **Jhana is recognisable as a conjunction**: strong pleasure, self-sustaining, absorbed,
   bright, lasting. Relaxation, dullness and effortful pleasure each miss a specific one.
7. **The brain data is thin and confounded.** On open recordings of 26 experts the published
   EEG signatures appear weakly, track smile-muscle activity, and do not separate jhana from
   ordinary concentration across people.

## How much to trust this

| Part | Basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| The loop and "do nothing" | Convergent teacher instructions; an interview study [S16]; consistent first-person accounts | Fairly high |
| Grasping as the main blocker | The most frequent blocker in a small sample; named by teachers | Moderate |
| Experience does not predict success | A few accounts; provider statements without data | Moderate to low |
| The specific conditions and profiles | This project's inference | Low until tested |
| The numerical model | Illustrative | Not evidence |
| EEG conclusions | This project's analysis of real open data, one session per expert | Moderate for experts; says nothing about beginners |

The first-person evidence base is 20 accounts. The planned forum sweep did not complete.
