## Methods

- One session per subject from the open dataset (BIDS, 64-channel BioSemi, 256 Hz, average reference). Segments are defined by the participants' own button presses.
- 8-second epochs, starting 15 s after each marker; 1–45 Hz band-pass; epochs with peak-to-peak above 150 µV on the analysed channels dropped; at most 36 evenly spaced epochs per subject and condition.
- Broadband and band power by Welch's method; aperiodic slope as a log-log fit over 2–40 Hz excluding 7–14 Hz; Lempel-Ziv (1976) complexity of the median-binarised signal at 128 Hz, normalised by n/log2(n), averaged over 16 scalp sites (64-ch) or the four sites (4-ch).
- Baseline = eyes-closed day-planning, mental arithmetic or mind-wandering, recorded before and after the sit.
- No correction for multiple comparisons: the three published signatures were the planned tests; alpha and gamma are descriptive.

## Caveats

- All participants are experts who enter jhana at will. Nothing here shows what a beginner's first entry looks like.
- State labels are self-reports. Order is fixed (baseline, access, J1…J4, baseline), so depth is confounded with time; the pre/post baseline control addresses drift only partly.
- Two teaching traditions are pooled (breath-based and loving-kindness-based).
- LZ complexity and spectral slope are mathematically related, and both are sensitive to muscle activity.
- One session per subject was used out of roughly three available; the rest of the dataset was not downloaded.
